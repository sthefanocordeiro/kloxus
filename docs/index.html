Writing is the last step, not the hard one.

Before the first sentence of an issue, someone has to do an editor's job. When you publish alone, that someone is you, every week.

  1. Watch the sourcesDozens of sites, feeds and newsletters.
  2. Drop the repeatsThe same story, republished by many outlets.
  3. Choose the story listWhat goes in, in what order, and why.
  4. Collect and checkLinks, facts, what is still unknown.
  5. WriteThe part your readers subscribed for.

Kloxus takes these four steps off your desk. This is where issues slip and publications stall.

How an issue gets made.

Most of the work is ordinary automation. The language model is used only where an editor's judgment is needed, and one human step decides what ships.

  1. CollectNew items from your RSS feeds and open pages, on your schedule.Automation
  2. Merge repeatsOne entry per story, listing every outlet that covered it.Automation
  3. ScoreA 0 to 10 relevance score against your editorial line, with a one-line reason.AI model
  4. Story listFive to ten items grouped by theme, each with why it matters.AI model
  5. DossierAn original summary, facts with links, a short quote, and what nobody knows yet.AI model, with a short research step
  6. DraftYour sections, filled in, with empty slots for your commentary.AI model
  7. Source checkAny factual sentence without a link is flagged and blocks sending.AI model and a hard rule
  8. Your reviewApprove, reject with a reason, edit, or ask for an alternative. You write the commentary.You
  9. SendEmail to your readers, or export to paste into Substack or beehiiv.Automation

Every rejection, with its reason, becomes a line in your editorial guide. Next week starts closer to your taste.

You can read and edit the guide at any time. No hidden profile.

Rules the product does not bend.

These are requirements, not taglines. They shape what the software is allowed to do.

Your byline, your words
Kloxus prepares the material. The commentary is always written by the person who signs the issue.
Nothing ships without you
Every issue needs your explicit approval before it is sent.
Every fact has a source
Each factual sentence links to where it came from. Unlinked sentences block sending.
Facts apart from opinion
Interpretation is labelled as interpretation, so readers never mistake a model's guess for a fact.
Curation, not copying
Original summaries, short quotes with credit, and only sources open to reading: RSS, public APIs, pages that allow it. Nothing behind a paywall.
Your data stays yours
Creator and reader data is not used to train models.

Readers pay you. Not us.

If you want to charge for an issue, readers subscribe through your own payment account. Kloxus only opens the door.

Your readerSubscribes with PIX or card
Your AbacatePay accountOpened and verified by you
YouReceive the full amount, minus the gateway's fee

Kloxus creates the subscription with a key you restrict, and unlocks the paid issue when the payment is confirmed. It never receives or holds the money and takes no commission.

Where the project stands.

Concept and specification

There is no working code yet. What exists today is the problem framing, the product design and a validation plan.

Before building further, the plan is to talk to independent creators and test the riskiest assumptions: that preparing an issue is the real bottleneck, that the suggested story list is good enough to keep, and that creators would pay for it.
