| D42 | 2026-11-16 | **Bounded dedupe (KLOX-46).** `internal/dedupe` no longer uses union-find over all pairs. Assignment is two-stage: exact canonical-URL groups (pre-chunked to the cap) then SimHash matching where an item joins a cluster only when its own hash sits within 4 bits of the cluster's FIRST item — later members never become match targets, so no transitive chain can form. Gates: ≥40 non-stopword body tokens (titles excluded — a republish's new headline must not defeat the match), publication times ≤72 h apart (`published_at` first, `fetched_at` fallback — the `safe_ts` rule), hard cap of 12 members for exact, SimHash and cited-signal merges alike; items without a parseable date can still exact-merge but never SimHash-merge. Candidate lookup is banded LSH (5 bands: 13+13+13+13+12 bits — distance ≤4 guarantees one equal band), not O(n²). Story titles name the earliest-published member article, not the longest title; signals can't supply titles. **Recorded realsample replies removed:** the `internal/realsample/llm` capture embodied the bug being fixed (~140 items in one union-find cluster; old 49 stories vs 191 under bounded dedupe), so no prompt-level rekey is possible — `TestRecordedRunReplays` now runs the captured feeds through the deterministic pipeline and a fresh real recording is an owner-only `record --live` action post-merge. The examples corpus rekeyed cleanly (identical 32-story partition; only one story title and one lengthened fixture body changed) by replaying recorded responses under the new request keys. | The old 16-bit threshold plus transitive union-find let ~1-in-20k chance matches chain 140+ unrelated items into a single story. Representative-only matching plus the size cap bounds every story by construction; banding keeps assignment well under the 5 s hosted budget at 5k items. The recorded sample's real capture can't be replayed under the corrected algorithm, and the sample is being reworked in group 7 anyway — deleting the stale replies keeps the test honest at zero cost. |
